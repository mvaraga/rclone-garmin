# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This application is a personal tool operated solely by its owner.

- It is used exclusively by the owner's own Google Account.
- It requests the `drive.file` scope, which grants access only to files
  the application itself creates in the owner's Google Drive.
- It does not access, collect, store, share or sell any other data, and
  it has no other users.
- Data handled by the application stays on the owner's own devices and
  in the owner's own Google Drive.
